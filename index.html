About ZeroWasteByte

Connecting Surplus Food with Those in Need Across Bengaluru.

Our Story

ZeroWasteByte was born from a simple observation: tonnes of nutritious food go to waste in PGs, hostels, and dining facilities while thousands in our city struggle for a single meal.

By coupling modern cloud microservices, MongoDB, and rapid real-time notifications, we empower donors to list food in seconds and NGOs to coordinate immediate, hygienic pickups.

How The System Works

1. List Surplus

PG owners upload surplus items with quantities, pickup times, and photos in 10 seconds.

2. Match & Claim

Verified nearby NGOs instantly claim the food through our live MongoDB backend.

3. Feed & Nourish

Food is safely collected and distributed to children, night shelters, and daily wage families.
