About ZeroWasteByte

Connecting Surplus Food with Those in Need Across Bengaluru.

Our Story

ZeroWasteByte was born from a simple observation: perfectly good food going to waste in PGs and restaurants while many in our city struggle with hunger. We believed technology could bridge this gap. Our journey started with a commitment to create a seamless, efficient platform to redirect surplus food, transforming potential waste into valuable meals.

We are driven by the vision of a Bengaluru where no one goes hungry and no food goes to waste.

The Challenge

Food waste is a significant environmental and social issue. In Bengaluru alone, tonnes of edible food are discarded daily from various sources, including PGs. Simultaneously, food insecurity affects vulnerable populations across the city. Coordinating the logistics of collecting and distributing surplus food quickly and safely is complex.

Our Solution

ZeroWasteByte provides a real-time platform connecting food donors (like PGs) with registered NGOs. Our system facilitates quick notifications, efficient claims, and coordinated pickups. By leveraging technology, we streamline the food rescue process, ensuring that surplus food reaches those who need it most, minimizing waste and maximizing impact.

How It Works

1. List Surplus

PG Owners & restaurants list their surplus food on our platform in seconds.

2. Connect Instantly

Verified NGOs in the area are immediately notified about the available food.

3. Feed the Needy

The NGO claims the food, picks it up, and distributes it to people who need it most.
